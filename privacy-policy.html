Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dozy: Baby Sleep Tracker (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in the Dozy: Baby Sleep Tracker app. We built Dozy: Baby Sleep Tracker to be private by design: your baby’s sleep data stays on your device, and we collect the minimum needed to run the app.

1. Information You Provide

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a user identifier and, only if your provider shares it, your email address. This is used to secure your account and sync your profile. We also store a few account flags: the App version you are using, whether you finished onboarding, the onboarding goals you picked (for example tracking wake windows), whether you opened the App today, whether you logged a sleep today, how many child profiles you created, your subscription status and plan (weekly or yearly), and (if you subscribe) the date and time of that subscription in your time zone.
  • Baby and sleep data. Your child’s profile (such as name and avatar), sleep records, notes, reminders, and app settings are entered by you. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Subscription status. To unlock Dozy: Baby Sleep Tracker Premium we receive your purchase status from Apple. We never see your full payment details.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not collect device advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or any baby/sleep data on our servers.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device. All baby and sleep data is stored locally using Apple’s on-device storage. If you use the same Apple ID across devices, this data may be backed up by iCloud under your Apple account, governed by Apple’s privacy terms.
  • Authentication profile. A profile document (user id, email if provided, sign-in provider, timestamps, and the account flags listed above) is stored with Google Firebase to manage your sign-in. Sleep records, notes, and other baby details are not sent to this service.

4. How We Use Information

We use your information only to:

  • authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • provide and maintain the App’s features;
  • verify and restore your subscription;
  • respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, on-device storage).
  • Google (Sign-In) and Google Firebase (authentication and the minimal profile document).

These providers process data only to perform the functions described above.

6. Children’s Privacy

Dozy: Baby Sleep Tracker is intended for use by parents and caregivers aged 18 or older. Information about a child is entered by the adult who uses the App, and that data remains on the adult’s device. The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your baby and sleep data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

You can delete your account at any time from the App’s settings. Deleting your account removes your authentication profile, deletes the local baby/sleep data owned by that account, and removes your account from our sign-in provider.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Because your baby/sleep data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly within the App. For account-level requests, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including the security features provided by Apple and our authentication provider. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Transfers

Our authentication provider may process the minimal profile data on servers located in other countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards are applied to such transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be communicated through the App.
